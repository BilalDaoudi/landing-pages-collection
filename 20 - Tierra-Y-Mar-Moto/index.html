About us

Our Company

Tierra Y Mar Moto is a dynamic and versatile company specializing in the sales, rentals, and tours of motorcycles. Committed to delivering an unparalleled experience for motorcycle enthusiasts, our business is founded on a passion for adventure, exploration, and the thrill of the open road. With a diverse fleet of high-quality motorcycles and a team of knowledgeable professionals, we cater to both seasoned riders and those embarking on their first biking adventure.

Our Tourn

Iksane Village Historical

Immerse yourself in history with the Iksane Village Historical Tour, a professionally guided motorcycle expedition that explores the rich cultural heritage of Sotolazar. Witness the ancient Spanish iron furnaces in the village of Iksane, where time seems to stand still. Marvel at well-preserved monuments and immerse yourself in the tales of bygone eras. This tour is designed for history enthusiasts and those eager to witness the legacy of Spanish influence on this charming village. Explore the past with Tierra Y Mar Moto's Iksane Village Historical Tour.

Boukana Beach

Embark on the Boukana Beach Tour with Tierra Y Mar Moto for an exhilarating coastal adventure. This professionally guided journey takes you along scenic routes with breathtaking ocean views, exploring hidden coves and pristine beaches. Riders will experience the thrill of the open road while discovering the charm of coastal towns and enjoying stops at iconic beachside destinations.

Mountain Gorgo

Conquer the majestic Mountain Gorgo on this expertly curated tour by Tierra Y Mar Moto. This tour caters to riders seeking an adrenaline-pumping mountainous adventure. Wind through challenging yet picturesque roads as you ascend the peaks, taking in panoramic views of valleys and forests. Our professional guides ensure a safe and unforgettable journey through the heart of nature's grandeur.
